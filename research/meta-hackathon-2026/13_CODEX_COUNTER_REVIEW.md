## 5. What holds up

**CHECKED / MEASURED — verified strengths, not courtesy agreement:**

- The guide hash comparison, building window, submission conversion, rubric weights, public-repo/new-work/rights requirements, card prerequisite and discretionary partner credits hold up (§1). The equal-weight sentence on the event page concerns application review; it does not contradict the captured Stage Two weights.
- The corrected per-concept SAM cost arithmetic is sound, and distinguishes tracking frame rate from final-output frame rate. The reservation broker's atomic reservation and retaining reservations after a crash are sensible designs, although no implementation or hard bound was tested.
- Claude correctly rebuts RT02's absolute claim that SAM cannot select one instance: box/point prompts are documented. The need to test automatic Spark grounding remains. Newly located SAM documentation also resolves several formerly undocumented tracking details.
- Withdrawal of the C4 lean, disclosure of uncertainty and explicit INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE outcome improve the proposal. The broad marketing claims should remain conditional even after smoke tests pass.
- Owned staged props, synthetic sites, consent records, Standard-mode selection, restricting clue outputs and separating human review are useful safety reductions. None alone establishes privacy-law compliance or absence of misuse.
- The audit response's separation of machine, model, observed AT and human evidence is sound. Grouping benchmark variants by base document, blinding labels, reporting abstentions and rejecting self-grading as independent verification address real sources of error. Independence remains specific to the claim, not a universally ordered L0–L4 quality scale.
- [PDF Association guidance](https://pdfa.org/accessible-math-in-pdf-finally/) supports the PDF/UA distinction: “PDF/UA-1 rules, which require, among other things, Formula tags to have alternative text.” Keeping navigable MathML in an HTML twin is coherent with a PDF/UA-1 output target. It does not imply MathML is impossible in all PDFs; supported PDF 2.0/PDF/UA-2 workflows exist.
- The revised D19 wording agrees across the draft's authority sections. The task-spec, decision log, source provenance and handoff formats are useful drift controls. No evidence shows they guarantee drift-free switching or enforce every approval gate.
